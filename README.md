# AI-Change-Impact-Analyzer
#### AI-assisted decision support for organizational change impact analysis, risk identification, and structured action planning using the CIRA framework.

## Overview
Organizational changes rarely affect a single process or system. A seemingly small change can create downstream impacts across people, workflows, applications, data, documentation, and interconnected dependencies. Identifying these ripple effects often requires manual investigation across fragmented information, making impact analysis time-consuming, difficult to scale, and vulnerable to overlooked dependencies.

The AI Change Impact Analyzer is a proposed AI-assisted solution designed to help Business Analysts, Product Managers, Project Managers, and Digital Transformation teams identify potential change impacts, surface associated risks, and organize follow-up actions in a structured, reviewable workflow.

Using the CIRA (Change Impact & Risk Analysis) framework, the product structures analysis around four stages: Change, Impact, Risk, and Action. It aims to turn an initial change description into categorized impact findings, risk assessments, supporting rationale, confidence indicators, and recommended validation or mitigation actions.

The product is designed to augment—not replace—professional judgment. AI-generated findings require human review and validation before they inform business or implementation decisions.


## Proposed Value
1. **Broader impact visibility**: Examine potential effects across processes, people, systems, data, documentation, and dependencies.
2. **Structured risk identification**: Organize potential risks by severity, confidence, and rationale.
3. **Actionable analysis**: Connect findings to investigation, validation, mitigation, and monitoring activities.
4. **Human-in-the-loop decision support**: Keep analysts in control through reviewable findings and explicit validation statuses.
5. **A scalable analysis approach**: Establish a repeatable framework that can evolve from a prototype toward a functional MVP.

## Product Direction
The initial focus is to establish a reliable core workflow, define structured outputs, and evaluate the quality and usefulness of AI-generated findings. Future development can explore richer organizational context, persistence, and integrations based on validation and user needs.

_This project explores how AI can improve the consistency, visibility, and usability of change impact analysis. The proposed capabilities and benefits are product objectives, not claims of validated production performance.
